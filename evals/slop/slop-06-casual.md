Here's what nobody tells you about learning to cook: the recipes are the easy part.

I spent a year following instructions perfectly and still turning out mediocre dinners. What surprised me most was how little the measurements mattered. I was fascinated to discover that timing beats precision every single time.

Think about it: when did a great cook ever measure the garlic?

It's not a technique thing. Not a gear thing. Not even a talent thing. A patience thing.

Salt. That's it. That's the secret. Taste the food, add salt, taste again, and stop when it's good. And that's okay.

I burned a lot of onions figuring this out. But in the end, cooking was never really about the food.
